# Notion Workflow

Use this skill after any material product, architecture, research, implementation, friction, feature-request, or demo change.

## Pages

- `v2` — canonical product/architecture/research
- `Product Exploration — Feature Base` — exploratory ideas and alternatives
- `Dev + Friction Log` — build evidence, friction, feedback, feature requests
- `Demo Video` — real demo pipeline
- `Agent Operating System` — agent process rules

## Rules

- Do not create parallel local project-state logs.
- Append/correct in Notion during ideation.
- Preserve older assumptions and add corrections rather than silently erasing context.
- Never claim Notion was updated unless the connector call succeeded.
- Keep official source links with material research findings.

## Completion

A material task is not complete until the relevant Notion page is updated.
